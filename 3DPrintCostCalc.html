Calculation assumptions: Filament weight includes the material you enter, including supports if applicable. The failure allowance models independent failed attempts with the entered failure probability. Design costs are allocated evenly across the specified quantity. Profit margin is calculated on selling price, after marketplace fees. GST, shipping, marketplace-specific fixed charges, financing, and discounts are not separately modelled in V1. The figures are estimates, not a tax invoice. Values are saved locally in this browser.
